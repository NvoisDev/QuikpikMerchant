Here’s a design breakdown of the UI, focusing on what’s working well, where visual cognitive load can be reduced, and practical recommendations to make it feel sharper.
What’s Working Well
 Clear Card Separation: Border radius and subtle shadows establish a distinct structure for each product.
 Good Visual Cues: Use of status tags like Active (green) and visual tags for category/pack sizes helps identify quick metadata.
 Scannable Metrics: Grouping key numerical figures (Price, MOQ, Stock, Margin %) horizontally makes comparative scanning relatively fast.
Key Areas for Improvement
1. Visual Hierarchy & Clutter
 Too Many Badge Styles: Within the top section of a single card, there are solid green pills (Active), light green pills (Grains & Swallows), outline pills (20 x 1kg), and visual icons. This creates badge fatigue.
 Stacked Stock Details: Under the "Stock" column, having ⁠44 units⁠, ⁠44 packs⁠, ⁠1 batch⁠, and ⁠44% sold⁠ stacked vertically creates a heavy text block. The orange percentage for "sold" draws attention, but it's hard to parse alongside light grey sub-text.
2. Alignment & Spacing
 Inconsistent Header Row: The shopping bag icon next to the title feels disconnected, and the titles wrap awkwardly near the icon.
 Action Icons at Bottom: The pencil, cube+, and "1 batch" action links at the bottom left are tiny and have low contrast/touch-target area, making them harder to tap on mobile.
3. Top Navigation Bar
 Crowded Top Bar: Having six header actions (hamburger menu, box, plus, share, bell, logo) in a small screen space leaves very little room for the page title.
Actionable Recommendations
Design Refinement Suggestions
1. Consolidate Tags & Badges:
 Keep status (Active) prominent.
 Move category/pack tags into plain text or secondary metadata row rather than full pill buttons.
2. Clean Up Stock & Batch Metrics:
 Group related metrics cleanly: e.g., combine unit/pack info into one line (⁠44 units (44 packs)⁠) or place progress indicators (like ⁠% sold⁠) in a subtle progress bar.
3. Improve Action Area:
 Give primary card actions (Edit, Add Batch) clearer text labels or put them in an overflow menu (⁠⋮⁠) on the top-right of each card to save vertical space.
4. Mobile Layout Optimization:
 Increase touch target sizes for mobile (minimum 44x44 pt for icons).
 Simplify the top app bar by nesting secondary actions into a menu.
