1. Wholesale operations management
2. Wholesale supplier discovery

“Quikpik is the operating system for modern wholesalers.”

* Modern SaaS feel
* Premium and trustworthy

* Mobile responsive
* Faster perceived performance
* Better conversion to sign-up/demo
* Stronger visual storytelling
* Blend operations + discovery naturally
