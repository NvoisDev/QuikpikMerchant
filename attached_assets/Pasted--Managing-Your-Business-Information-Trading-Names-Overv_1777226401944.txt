🧾 Managing Your Business Information & Trading Names
Overview

Quikpik allows you to operate under one legal business while presenting different trading names (brands) to your customers.

This is useful if you:

Sell under multiple brand names
Serve different customer types
Want different invoice branding
🏢 Your Legal Business Information

Your legal business is the official company responsible for:

Payments
Tax (VAT)
Invoicing
Compliance
You’ll need to provide:
Legal Business Name
VAT Number (if applicable)
Company Registration Number (if applicable)
Registered Address

👉 This information will appear on all invoices for compliance.

🏷️ What Are Business Profiles?

Business Profiles are your trading names (also known as “doing business as”).

Examples:

Fresh Foods Ltd (legal company)
Trading as:
City Grocers
Urban Fresh Supplies
🔄 How It Works
You operate one account, one stock system, one warehouse
You can create multiple business profiles (if enabled)
When creating a quote or invoice, you can choose which name to display
🧾 What Customers See

On invoices and quotes, customers will see:

Your selected business profile (brand name)
Your legal business details (for compliance)

Example:

City Grocers
Trading as: Fresh Foods Ltd
VAT: 123456789

⚙️ How to Set It Up
1. Add Your Legal Business Details

Go to:
Settings → Business Information

Fill in:

Legal name
VAT number
Address
2. Create Business Profiles (if enabled)

Go to:
Settings → Business Profiles

You can:

Add new profiles
Set a default profile
Edit names and branding
3. Select Profile When Creating Quotes

When creating a quote or order:

Choose your Business Profile from the dropdown
This determines what your customer sees
⚠️ Important Notes
All payments go to the same business account
Business profiles are for branding only
You cannot use different VAT numbers under one account
Your legal business remains responsible for all transactions
❓ Need Help?

If you want to enable multiple business profiles or need help setting this up, contact support via the admin panel.

💡 Tip

Use different profiles to:

Separate premium vs budget product lines
Brand differently for different customers
Keep your operations simple while presenting flexibility
