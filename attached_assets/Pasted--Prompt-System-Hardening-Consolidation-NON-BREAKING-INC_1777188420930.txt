🧱 PART 3: Quote Expiry (STOCK PROTECTION)
Problem

Stock deducted before payment → can block inventory

Add (additive only):
orders.quote_expires_at (nullable)
orders.is_expired (boolean)
Logic:
On quote creation:
quote_expires_at = now + defined window (e.g. 2 hours)
On expiry:
Mark as expired
Restore stock
Safety
Apply ONLY to new quotes
Do NOT retroactively modify old orders
