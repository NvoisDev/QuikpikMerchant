I want to elevate the Quikpik product page UI to feel more like a modern B2B commerce intelligence platform rather than just a catalog listing.

Current design foundation is good, but I want the experience to feel more premium, data-driven, and interactive.

Goals:

* Make product pages feel intelligent and dynamic
* Improve trust and marketplace credibility
* Introduce lightweight analytics visuals
* Reduce empty space and improve visual hierarchy
* Make Quikpik feel like the operating system for wholesale commerce

Requested improvements:

1. Product Hero Section

* Reduce excess whitespace around product image
* Add image carousel/swipe support
* Add subtle shadows/gradient background
* Support product badges:
    * Verified Supplier
    * Trending
    * Fast Moving
    * Low Stock

2. Stock & Demand Intelligence
    Replace static stock cards with insight-driven cards:

* Current stock
* Sales velocity
* Demand trend
* Tiny sparkline/line graph visual
* Example:
    “8 units left”
    “Selling faster this week ↑18%”

3. Modern Analytics UI
    Incorporate modern line graph / sparkline design patterns similar to fintech dashboards:

* Smooth curved lines
* Soft gradients
* Minimal axis clutter
* Interactive hover states
* Compact embedded graphs inside cards

4. CTA Hierarchy
    Primary CTA:

* Request Invoice

Secondary CTA:

* WhatsApp Supplier

Additional lightweight actions:

* Save Product
* Share Product
* Track Price

5. Trust & Marketplace Signals
    Add support for:

* Verified wholesaler badge
* Delivery coverage
* Response time
* Repeat buyer %
* Fulfilment speed
* Retailer trust indicators

6. Motion & Micro-interactions
    Add subtle:

* hover states
* graph animations
* skeleton loaders
* button depth
* smooth transitions

7. Overall Direction
    The UI direction should evolve toward:
    “Bloomberg / Stripe / Linear / modern fintech analytics”
    combined with
    “B2B wholesale marketplace”

The experience should feel:

* premium
* intelligent
* trustworthy
* data-rich
* operationally powerful

Focus heavily on mobile responsiveness and clean card-based layouts.
