This step is VERY important because this is where Quikpik starts becoming a real B2B commerce platform rather than just software.

You want the enquiry flow to:

feel lightweight
not overwhelm retailers
collect enough information for wholesalers to qualify leads
create serious business opportunities

The mistake most platforms make is asking for too much too early.

Best Structure for “Make Contact / Enquiry”
1. Business Information (Essential)
Business Name
Business / Store Name
Contact Person
Full Name
Phone Number

Very important in wholesale.

Email Address

Needed for formal communication.

2. Business Type (Very Valuable)

Dropdown:

Retail Store
Supermarket
Convenience Store
Restaurant
Distributor
Cash & Carry
Online Store
Market Trader
Other

This becomes valuable later for:

analytics
supplier targeting
lead quality
3. Location
Address / Postcode

Very important for:

delivery feasibility
territory coverage
route planning later

You could even reuse your Google Places feature here.

4. Products Interested In

Very important.

Options:

Free text
OR
Auto-filled from selected products

Example:

Interested in:
- Indomie
- Frozen Chicken
- Malt Drinks
5. Estimated Order Volume (VERY IMPORTANT)

This helps wholesalers qualify serious buyers.

Simple dropdown:

Estimated Order Size
Under £100
£100–£500
£500–£1,000
£1,000+
Regular weekly orders

This is GOLD for wholesalers.

6. Message Box
Tell the wholesaler what you're looking for

Example:

Looking for weekly supply for our convenience store in Birmingham.
7. Preferred Contact Method
Phone
WhatsApp
Email

VERY important in wholesale.

Honestly:
WhatsApp may become dominant for your users.

8. Optional Delivery Questions

Later:

Need delivery?
Collection only?
Frequency needed?

But not necessary yet.

What Happens After Submission?

This part matters just as much.

Wholesaler should receive:

email
dashboard notification
optional WhatsApp alert later

With:

New wholesale enquiry from XYZ Stores

Then inside Quikpik:

lead pipeline
contacted/not contacted
converted/not converted

Eventually this becomes:

wholesale CRM.

VERY Important Strategic Point

Do NOT force pricing transparency yet.

Many wholesalers:

negotiate pricing
tier pricing
custom quotes
relationship pricing

So:

Request Quote

is smarter than:

Buy Now

at this stage.

What I Think Your Real Opportunity Is

You are quietly building:

B2B wholesale infrastructure

Not just:

inventory software

That’s a much bigger category long term.

My Strong Recommendation

Start SIMPLE:

✅ enquiry forms
✅ storefronts
✅ retailer discovery

Avoid immediately adding:
❌ carts
❌ checkout
❌ live marketplace logistics
❌ escrow systems
❌ public pricing wars

The lightweight network model is much safer and more scalable initially.
