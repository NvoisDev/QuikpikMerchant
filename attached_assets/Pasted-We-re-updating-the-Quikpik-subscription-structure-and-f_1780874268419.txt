We’re updating the Quikpik subscription structure and feature access model. The goal is to introduce a dedicated supplier listing/discovery tier while keeping the current operational SaaS structure stable for existing users.
IMPORTANT:
This rollout must NOT break:
existing live users
Stripe subscriptions
feature permissions
onboarding flow
current operational access
The implementation should use clean feature gating and tier-based permission checks.
UPDATED TIER STRUCTURE
Listing Tier (NEW FREE PLAN)
Purpose:
A lightweight supplier visibility plan focused on marketplace/discovery access only.
Pricing: £19.99/month or £199.90/year (Save 16% / £39.98/year)
Introductory Price is free — valid for 3 months
Existing users maintain their current subscription
Limits:
Up to 10 products
Up to 2 price lists
1 user account
Includes:
Public supplier profile
Product listings
Marketplace/discovery visibility
Retailer enquiries/leads
Search visibility
Basic supplier dashboard
Restrictions:
No invoices
No payments
No analytics
No advanced stock management
No batch tracking
No customer management
No operational workflows
No draft invoices
No reports
No order management
Suggested positioning:
“Get discovered by retailers”

Pricing: £29.99/month or £299.90/year (Save 16% / £59.98/year)

Limits:
Up to 20 products
Up to 5 price lists
1 user account
Includes:

Analytics
Customer management
Order management

Basic reporting
