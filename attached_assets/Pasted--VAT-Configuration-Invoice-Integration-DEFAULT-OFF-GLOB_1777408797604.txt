🧾 VAT Configuration & Invoice Integration (DEFAULT OFF, GLOBAL-READY, NON-BREAKING)

---

## 🎯 Objective

Introduce VAT handling across the platform with:

* VAT disabled by default
* Wholesalers can enable VAT if required
* Default VAT rate = 20% when enabled
* Fully editable VAT settings
* Accurate VAT calculation on invoices and orders

---

## ⚠️ Core Rules

* Do NOT break existing orders or totals
* Do NOT retroactively modify historical orders
* VAT must be calculated at order creation and stored
* VAT settings belong to the wholesaler (not global)

---

## 🧱 Part 1 — Data Model

Extend wholesaler/business profile:

* vatEnabled (boolean, default: false)
* vatRate (number, default: 0.20)
* vatNumber (string, optional)

---

## 🧠 Part 2 — VAT Logic

At order creation:

IF vatEnabled = true:

* vatAmount = subtotal × vatRate

ELSE:

* vatAmount = 0

Total:

total = subtotal + vatAmount + customerTransactionFee (if applicable)

👉 VAT is calculated once and stored on the order

---

## 🧾 Part 3 — Invoice Updates

Update invoice layout to include (ONLY if VAT enabled):

* Subtotal
* VAT (e.g. “VAT (20%)”)
* Total (including VAT)

Also include:

* VAT number (if provided)

If VAT disabled:

* Do NOT show VAT line at all

---

## 🧍 Part 4 — Business Settings UI

Section: “Tax Settings”

Fields:

* Toggle: “VAT Registered”

  * Default: OFF

* VAT Rate (%)

  * Default: 20
  * Editable

* VAT Number (optional)

---

## 🎯 UX Behaviour

* When user toggles VAT ON:

  * Automatically populate VAT rate = 20%
* User can edit rate if needed

---

## 🌍 Global Flexibility

* Support:

  * 0% VAT
  * Different VAT rates per wholesaler
* Do NOT hardcode UK logic anywhere else

---

## 🧪 Backward Compatibility

* Existing orders:

  * VAT = 0
  * No changes

* New orders:

  * VAT applied only if enabled

---

## ⚠️ Safety

* Always use stored vatAmount (never recalculate old orders)
* Ensure VAT is not applied to platform/customer fees
* Prevent double counting

---

## 📦 Deliverables

* VAT toggle in business settings
* VAT applied to new orders when enabled
* Clean invoice display with VAT breakdown
* No regressions in totals or payments

---

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria

* VAT is OFF by default
* Can be enabled and edited easily
* Invoices reflect VAT correctly
* Totals are accurate
* System remains stable

---

## 🧠 Guiding Principle

“VAT should be invisible unless needed, and accurate when enabled.”
