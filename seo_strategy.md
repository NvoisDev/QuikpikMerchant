# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Blog index and blog posts
- Public wholesaler storefront pages
- Public product pages
- Public wholesaler welcome / onboarding pages
- Legal pages that are publicly accessible

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard routes
- Admin pages (`/admin`, `/super-admin`)
- Internal wholesaler tools behind login

## Target audience
- Wholesalers looking for inventory, order, customer, and payment management software
- Retailers or business buyers discovering public storefronts and products

- Wholesale order management
- Wholesale customer portal
- Wholesale product marketplace
